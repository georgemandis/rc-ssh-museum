RC MuSSHeum is an art gallery accessible over SSH,
hosted at the Recurse Center hub.
You'll need an SSH key and a Recurse Center account to enter.
On first visit, you'll be asked to verify your RC identity
through a one-time OAuth flow in your browser.
Once verified, your SSH key is remembered and you can
visit anytime without re-authenticating.
